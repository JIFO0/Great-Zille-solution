# The Great Zille Solution

A modification for *Suzerain* (*Kingdom of Rizia* DLC) that adds a military path to resolve the crisis over the Zille province occupied by Wehlen under Wiktor Smolak.

## Overview

* **Military alternative for Zille:** Players are no longer limited to the diplomatic route (AN referendum) or intelligence operations. You can plan, prepare, and launch a military invasion.
* **Timeline integration:** Military options and staff preparations appear starting from Turn 1, with key moments in Turns 2, 3 (the Zille bombing), 4, 8, and 9.
* **New 3D war theater:** A dedicated military operation featuring tactical tiles around the Zille peninsula and covering cities in the Brenas province.
* **Internal politics and geopolitics:** Rivalry between House Azaro and House Sazon over war logistics, pacts with Lespia (Patricio Alvarez) and Derdia (Azmal), and reactions from major powers (CSP, Valgsland, Contana).

## War Theater Cities

### Rizia Starting Cities (Brenas Province)
* **Marbelas** – border fortress and supply base
* **Dapalle** – main transport hub of Brenas
* **Mave Nortus** – eastern gate and valley

### Wehlen-Controlled Cities (Zille Peninsula)
* **Zille** – provincial capital, heavily fortified administrative and oil center
* **Wiktrany** – central transport hub of the peninsula, key to logistics
* **Munat** – deep-water seaport in the north, supply point from Wehlen
* **Nanta** – eastern flank, border bases

## War Outcomes and Peace Treaties

The outcome of the military operation and the peace negotiation terms with Wiktor Smolak depend on city control at the end of the fighting:

### 1. Total Fiasco
* **Map conditions:** Wehlen breaks through the defensive line and captures all 3 cities in the Brenas province (Marbelas, Dapalle, Mave Nortus).
* **Consequences:** Smolak occupies southern Brenas and dictates terms without negotiation. Financial paralysis (-5 Budget, Energy drain) and a military coup or dethronement of Romus.

### 2. Tactical Defeat
* **Map conditions:**
  * Status Quo: all starting Rizian cities in Brenas remain under Rizian control, and all Wehlen cities on the Zille peninsula remain under Wehlen control.
  * Or the loss of 1 city in Brenas without offsetting it with gains in Wehlen.
* **Consequences:** As the initiating side, Rizia suffers a strategic and political failure despite heavy budget costs, casualties, and international criticism. This leads to forced negotiations, Wehlen demanding reparations or concessions, and Rizia having to renounce claims to Zille to avoid rebellion and internal collapse.

### 3. Bloody Stalemate
* **Map conditions:**
  * Territorial exchange: Rizia loses 1 of its starting cities in Brenas, but captures 1 or 2 Wehlen-controlled cities (e.g., Wiktrany, Nanta, or the port of Munat).
  * Or capturing part of the peninsula without taking the capital of Zille while holding its own borders.
* **Consequences:** Both armies are exhausted, the front freezes, and war weariness grows.
* **Special negotiation path — Zille Independence:** At the negotiating table, Romus can reach a compromise where Zille gains full independence as a neutral buffer state outside Smolak's control. This requires some concessions to Wehlen (such as trade and oil preferences, resource transit, or limited financial compensation).

### 4. Decisive Victory
* **Map conditions:** Full liberation of all 4 cities on the Zille peninsula (Zille, Wiktrany, Munat, Nanta) while defending the Brenas cities.
* **Consequences:** Smolak is forced to sign a treaty without the AN or a referendum. Zille returns directly to the Crown of Rizia as a full province of the kingdom.

### 5. Overwhelming Triumph
* **Map conditions:** Liberation of all of Zille plus capturing strategic targets deep inside Wehlen territory (e.g., Port Rotsla).
* **Consequences:** Wehlen pays heavy war reparations (+4 Budget, +3 Energy), and Grodzizg becomes a Free City under the protectorate of Rizia and Derdia.
